Creating a task in most project tools means filling in 11 fields. Nobody does that properly, so the data goes stale and the dashboards lie.

For StructFlow, a workspace for a structural design firm, I made the title field do the work:

"Column schedule Prestige Towers @Amit !high due fri 8h"

One line fills project, assignee, priority, due date, estimate and type. The part I cared about most is trust, not speed:

→ Every auto-filled field is labelled, and "Why these?" shows the reason for each one
→ When it can't tell which project a task belongs to, it flags the field instead of guessing
→ Projects carry contract value, so a misread ₹2.5Cr gets spelled out and confirmed. Tasks don't get that friction.
→ Every create ends in a toast with Undo, not a modal that just closes

I designed and built it AI-native with Claude, from research PDFs to a working prototype, in two days. Full case study, with a clickable prototype, is in my portfolio. Link in the comments.
